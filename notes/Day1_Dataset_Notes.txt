Day 1: Dataset Selection & Understanding
Project: Supermarket Sales Power BI Project

1. Dataset Source:
   - Name: Supermarket Sales Dataset
   - Source: Kaggle (or your downloaded file)
   - File format: CSV
   - Number of rows: ~1,000 
   - Number of columns: 17 

2. Column Descriptions:
   - Invoice ID: Unique transaction ID
   - Branch: Store branch (A, B, C)
   - City: City of the branch
   - Customer Type: Member or Normal
   - Gender: Customer gender
   - Product Line: Category of product sold
   - Unit Price: Price per item
   - Quantity: Number of items sold
   - Tax 5%: Tax amount on sales
   - Total: Total sales amount (Unit Price * Quantity + Tax)
   - Date: Transaction date
   - Time: Transaction time
   - Payment: Payment method (Cash, Credit, Ewallet)
   - cogs: Cost of goods sold
   - gross margin percentage: Gross margin %
   - gross income: Gross profit per transaction
   - Rating: Customer satisfaction rating

3. Initial Observations:
   - Dates are in dd/mm/yyyy format
   - Numeric columns: Unit Price, Quantity, Tax, Total, cogs, gross income
   - Categorical columns: Branch, City, Customer Type, Gender, Product Line, Payment
   - Some columns may need renaming for clarity
   - No major missing values observed (to confirm in Power BI)

4. Potential Insights to Explore:
   - Total sales per product line
   - Sales trends over time (monthly/weekly)
   - Compare sales by branch and city
   - Average customer rating per product category
   - Analyze payment methods used
